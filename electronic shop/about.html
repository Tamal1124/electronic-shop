About ElectricHub

Our Story

Built on Trust & Quality

ElectricHub was founded in 2009 by Rajan Sharma, a trained electrical engineer who saw the need for a reliable, affordable electrical shop in the community. What started as a small 200 sq.ft shop has grown into a 2,000 sq.ft showroom trusted by thousands of customers.

Over the years, we have partnered with India's top electrical brands — Philips, Havells, Crompton, Legrand, Finolex, Polycab, Schneider and more — to bring you a curated selection of over 500 quality-certified products.

Our mission is simple: provide safe, high-quality electrical products at honest prices, backed by expert advice. Whether you are a homeowner wiring a new house, a contractor doing bulk procurement, or someone needing a single light bulb — we are here for you.
